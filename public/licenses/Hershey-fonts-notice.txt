Hershey fonts — acknowledgements that travel with the font data

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
U. S. National Bureau of Standards.

The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

LaTeX Modeler uses the shapes of these letters (converted to lists of points, see
scripts/hand-templates.mts) only to recognise handwriting. The data came from the
hersheytext package (MIT licence, see hersheytext-MIT.txt).
